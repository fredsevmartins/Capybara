# Code of conduct

Participate respectfully. Welcome scientific disagreement supported by evidence.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and unwanted personal contact are not
acceptable. Respect privacy and credit others' work. Maintainers may remove harmful
content, close discussions or restrict participation proportionally to the conduct.

Report concerns privately through GitHub's reporting tools or the repository owner's
available private contact route. Do not post private details in public issues.
Maintainers should handle reports confidentially and disclose conflicts of interest.
